Underground diamond core drilling for mine development, resource expansion,
and structural-stability assessments. Our underground crews work deep-hole,
directional, and geotechnical programmes to the standards expected of
production-drilling partners in Nordic mines.

## What we deliver

- Deep-hole directional drilling for resource-expansion programmes
- Geotechnical drilling for underground infrastructure and ground-support design
- Long-hole production-support campaigns coordinated with mine operators
- Crews certified to the mine-site access and safety programmes of the operators
  we drill for

## Operating area

Nordic mines across Sweden, Norway, and Finland.
