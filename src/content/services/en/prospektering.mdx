We drill with Odex 76 mm — a fast and cost-effective method. Moraine and
bedrock-chip samples are taken from the target depth. The casing is then
recovered cleanly so nothing is left in the ground.

The method is also suitable for filter wells, for example groundwater
sampling.

The rig is purpose-built for this type of sampling, and our crews have many
years of experience with the method.

References include Boliden, Agnico Eagle, and Golder Associates.
