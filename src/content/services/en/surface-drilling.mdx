NORSE operates a surface-drilling fleet built for Nordic ground. Diamond core
drilling supports mineral resource definition, geotechnical assessments, and
infrastructure projects — from greenfield programmes to brownfield expansions.

## What we deliver

- Resource-definition drilling for mineral exploration programmes
- Geotechnical campaigns for infrastructure and mining projects
- High-recovery core for reliable logging and assay
- Fully certified crews operating to Nordic safety and environmental standards

## Operating area

Sweden, Norway, and Finland. HQ and workshop in Malå, Sweden.
