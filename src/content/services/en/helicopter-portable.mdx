When a programme is sited on ground roads shouldn't touch — remote mountain
terrain, protected ecosystems, winter-only access — we fly the rig in instead.
Heli-portable drilling delivers full exploration campaigns with zero road
building and minimal ground disturbance.

## What we deliver

- Modular rigs broken down for standard helicopter sling loads
- Remote-camp operations with full logistical support
- Programmes in protected and sensitive environments where ground access is
  restricted or seasonal
- Environmentally responsible site-restoration after every campaign

## Operating area

Anywhere a Nordic helicopter can lift — currently deployed in Sweden, Norway,
and Finland.
